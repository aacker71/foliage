# Code of conduct

Foliage is a project about keeping an honest record of work. We ask the same of how we treat each other.

**Expected**
- Be kind, direct and specific. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you saw rather than what you guess someone meant.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's personal data, private repositories or credentials, including your own, in issues or pull requests.

**Not acceptable**
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure to merge or approve work.

**Scope.** This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

**Reporting and enforcement.** To report a problem privately, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/aacker71/foliage/security/advisories/new) and title it "Conduct report". Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules. Reports are handled in confidence.

This text is short on purpose. It is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (CC BY 4.0).
