# Foliage project overview

**Your work deserves a record.** Foliage is the living system of record for a builder's
work. The full owner-supplied [Language & Product Framing](product-thesis.md), captured
2026-09-23, is the product thesis to build from. Its text is preserved; the Library file's
remote identity has not been verified.

The private work record is the underlying product. Portfolios, résumés, reflections,
recruiting, client and coaching views are selective projections of that same record.
It should preserve work, decisions, contributions, outcomes, discoveries, learning,
meaning and evidence as they happen, including unfinished work and failed assumptions.
Achievements explain why work existed, what the person figured out, their contribution
and use of AI, and what changed. They organize meaningful work without forcing every
experiment into a success story. Activity volume is not demonstrated capability.

Success has two parts: the builder recognizes their work and understands what mattered;
an independent permitted reader can inspect the appropriate view and its supporting evidence.

## Goals

- Help the owner recognize meaningful work, grounded pride, interests and the builder's journey.
- Reduce maintenance through meaningful compression and optional daily/weekly reflection;
  preserve useful evidence and owner context instead of collecting activity for its own sake.
- Connect retained work to inspectable evidence and honest attribution.
- Explain the person and their achievements before asking readers to explore artifacts.
- Keep local collection private and make outward sharing a deliberate, reviewable action.
- Preserve one person's canonical Leaf across named company, role and project views.

The maintained record should be useful privately before distribution. Foliage is not centered
on a professional social feed or a routine of public self-promotion. Reflection and the full
automatic maintenance loop are product direction, not a claim of completed implementation.

## Core user flow

1. Select permitted sources; Photosynthesis captures and connects work as it happens.
2. Maintain the private record through existing candidate, achievement and citation operations;
   preserve owner corrections and separate observation, self-report, external validation and inference.
3. Offer daily/weekly reflection on decisions, progress, obstacles, discoveries and open questions;
   ask for missing personal context instead of making the owner reconstruct the work.
4. Project the relevant story into a portfolio, résumé or another selected audience view.
   The portfolio presents introduction, keystones and the useful compound-leaf timeline.
5. Review disclosure and publish an admitted view through Canopy under applicable authority.
6. Keep the record and its projections consistent; replace or withdraw publications with
   owner control, and keep deeper private history out of public views unless admitted.

This is the intended end-to-end flow, not a claim that every stage is integrated on
this branch. Consult [progress](progress-tracker.md) and [application ownership](architecture.md).

## Features and audiences

| Offer | Core outcome | Required scope |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | Retain and explain a person's work | Local sources, review, corrections, achievements, citations, media, repeat updates and portable exit |
| Individual Enterprise | See and retain permitted work within an employer | Subject-scoped views, policy explanations, correction, release requests and departure/export |
| Organization | Govern group-owned work | Teams, organizations, companies, explicit contributions, primary reporting placement, separate memberships and disclosure grants |
| Recruiter | Understand deliberately shared work | Public profile, achievement/evidence reading; separately consented, revocable discovery and subscriptions |
| Portfolio Hosting | Control a publication over time | Identity/identifier ownership, exact-content admission, private storage, read/replace/withdraw, recovery and paid-service lifecycle |

Cross-application scope includes separate private/public industry views, shared approval UX,
Novu inbox/email/SMS, scoped agent work, observability, recovery, support, entitlement/payment
handling and enterprise private deployment. These are accepted build requirements, not optional
extras removed by the current local focus. See the [complete-product acceptance matrix](../docs/PORTFOLIO-SCALE-ACCEPTANCE.md).

## Scope and success criteria

Individual, portfolio hosting, recruiter reading, organization administration and enterprise
member experiences each have an owner in the architecture inventory. Website, runtime and
legacy support applications are accounted for separately. A usable release must demonstrate
source selection → review → retained evidence → reader presentation with the real chosen data.
A published claim must not expose private support or imply independent outcome verification.

### Verify the outcome

- Use the founder's permitted multi-year Foliage work as the first demanding product test:
  preserve real pivots, assumptions, experiments, decisions, discoveries and outcomes;
  collect the founder's assessment of whether the record represents what the work took.
  Then have an independent permitted reader inspect a selected view and follow its claims
  to evidence. Neither reaction is assumed, fabricated, or inferred from a page-load test.
- A person can repeat collection/review without losing prior decisions, misreporting failures
  as empty work, or duplicating promoted evidence (LV01, ST04).
- A reader can move from introduction to an achievement, a specific claim and its retained
  support; missing support and observation dates remain explicit (OF01, OF04, AU04).
- A hosted customer can recover identity and perform exact-version upload, replace and
  withdrawal; current authority applies to every route and media request (OF05, ST01–ST03).
- An employee and administrator exercise separate authority, including correction and
  permitted departure export (OF02/OF03, AU01–AU03).
- Notifications, private deployment, recovery and commercial service meet their corresponding
  NT/OP acceptance rows. A local test or diagram alone does not satisfy those rows.

### Scope limits and implementation contracts

No invented career content, personal ranking, proof-tier score or ungranted public publication.
Current runtime gates require the person's publication action; the approved-project automatic
update direction below requires durable grants and revocation before it can change that behavior.
Desktop capture, portable evaluation and Dify development integration are included in the
[ordered units](specs/00-build-plan.md). Integrated evaluation/Dify code is reused;
native packaging, cloud isolation and the full live-evidence loop still need implementation
acceptance. Explicit immutable scenarios are supported by the specification. Authority for
unattended model-generated commands remains a separate, unresolved permission.

### Evidence and value language

Keep measured facts, attributed external feedback, self-reported reflection and AI inference
distinct. “Source of truth” names the owner-controlled record; it does not make every entry
objectively verified. Avoid upgrading a claim because the same source was summarized twice.
Work-value estimates may describe a bounded outcome using ranges, source data, assumptions,
method, timeframe and visible uncertainty. Do not convert them into a person's price, hiring
score, cross-person ranking or a numeric mapping of proof tiers. No estimate is needed when
the evidence cannot support one. This is product scope, not an implemented valuation engine.

### Open ontology refinement

The thesis allows a Leaf to describe a project, contribution, capability or journey; today's
schema uses Leaf for a person's canonical portfolio, with named views and achievement records.
Test the language against actual work before deciding whether existing views suffice or a
versioned migration is needed. Preserve current identities, citations, taxonomy and publication
compatibility meanwhile. Do not silently rename domain types or create duplicate work records.

## Portfolio harness proposal

The [Foliage portfolio harness specification](proposals/portfolio-harness.md) defines direct
OpenClaw/Hermes integration for Photosynthesis, authorized user-feedback discovery and
evidence-grounded synthesis. Coeadapt is the predecessor. The original draft is preserved;
the revision uses qualitative evidence, bounded verification and controlled publication.

## Launch decisions — 2026-09-21

First ten users: junior-to-mid developers with substantial open-source work, applying for
jobs. Readers: hiring managers, recruiting agents and prospective teammates. Initial launch
centers on an agent-prepared, owner-reviewed living portfolio with inspectable achievements;
career coaching follows the initial launch interpretation. Photosynthesis installation via
an npx marketplace flow is required, and OpenClaw is a required host. Other host launch
requirements remain unconfirmed.

The owner may opt into automatic updates for an approved project, including new impact claims
with attributed supporting feedback. This supersedes the blanket prohibition on autonomous
publication as product direction, but is not implemented authority: current review gates stay
until durable scoped grants, revocation and decision checks exist. TypeSafe Jev is selected for
structured decision routing; confidence does not prove a claim or confer publication consent.
Background scheduling cadence and enablement remain unresolved; do not silently enable them.

## Opportunity distribution — 2026-09-23

The live portfolio and resume must reach recruiters, hiring managers, prospective teams and
clients. Extend Canopy with opt-in discovery, tailored views/cited exports, authorized customer
ATS/CRM delivery and contracted data feeds. A client-facing view can present services and case
studies from the same achievements. These are extensions of existing offers, not a new ATS or
another portfolio store. [Distribution architecture](contracts/portfolio-distribution.md) owns
the scope and [units 35–37](specs/00-build-plan.md) own implementation acceptance.

First value is useful without vendor partnerships: a consenting developer's real work is
findable, shareable, readable and supports a wanted inquiry. Connected-system delivery follows
real customer permissions; Juicebox supplier ingestion requires separate agreement. Report
what happened (prepared, accepted, updated, inquiry), never promise a recruiter viewed a record
because an API accepted it. Automatic updates remain bounded by durable publication and
destination grants; no implied outreach, mass applications, fabricated impact or person scores.
