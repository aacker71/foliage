## What Foliage is

**The age of portfolios.** Career evidence, verifiable without a score.

```
CANOPY   the layer many trees form     → cloud plane, PROPRIETARY (the product sold)
  TREE     an organization             → tenancy, browsing, discovery
    LEAF     one person's portfolio    → OPEN SOURCE (theirs; inspectable or it's a promise)
      LEAFLET  one artifact of evidence → verified / pending / excluded (+ reason, always)
```

Pango protects the forest and maintains the Canopy. Each level has its caretaker agent — Pango › Picus › Dee Dee › Scarlet, with Fern
onboarding orgs — named and scoped in `docs/AGENT-ECOLOGY.md` (owner spec 2026-08-29).

## Invariants that decide design calls (owner decisions of record — not open questions)

1. **No score, ever, anywhere an employer can see.** No ranking, no cross-person
   aggregate, no ordered tiers. FCRA posture, decided 2026-08-05.
2. **No fake data.** Fixtures say so on their face; absence of the fabrication band is
   proven by differential control, never assumed.
3. **`excluded` is not an error** and always carries its reason.
4. **A false `verified` is the product-ending bug.** Verification claims get adversarial
   tests before they get pixels.
5. **The open set stands alone** (`pnpm check:boundary`), or "open source" is marketing.
