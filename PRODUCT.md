## Platform

web

## Users

The first pilot serves builders with substantial real work, initially junior-to-mid developers
with open-source contributions who are applying for jobs. They need to recover what they did,
understand why it mattered, and share a credible account without reconstructing years of work.
Hiring managers, recruiting agents and prospective teammates are initial readers of selected
public views. The builder owns the underlying private record.

## Product Purpose

Your work deserves a record. Foliage retains the work, decisions, discoveries, outcomes,
learning and evidence of a builder's journey while it happens. Success has two parts: the
builder recognizes what the work took and what mattered; an authorized independent reader can
follow a relevant claim to its supporting evidence. Daily and weekly reflection should compress
meaningful progress, not reward activity volume.

## Positioning

The private work record is the product. A living portfolio, résumé, reflection, recruiting,
client or coaching view is a selective projection of that same record. Foliage turns abundant
output into credible signal through provenance, owner correction and evidence-backed context.
It is not a social feed or a generated brag sheet.

## Operating Context

Photosynthesis collects from sources the owner permits and prepares review candidates.
Leaflets retain smaller units of meaningful work; the canonical Leaf records the person's
account and its cited achievements. Scarlet may help prepare wording under a current consent
and review path. Canopy admits only the selected public projection and supports replacement
and withdrawal. Work may remain unfinished, uncertain, excluded or private. A published view
must not silently reveal the deeper private journey.

The first local pilot runs in the builder's browser from an installed local runtime. The public
website introduces the product and offers the current pilot package. The local package and the
separately configured hosted service have distinct capabilities; consult the progress tracker
before claiming either is accepted in production.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The user may change their own account, evidence, presentation and sharing choices; the
  system should reduce routine maintenance without publishing or inventing work for them.
- Citations, attribution, proof state and uncertainty must remain inspectable. Measured fact,
  external validation, self-report and AI inference are different kinds of support.
- No personal score, proof-tier ranking, fabricated work, fake impact or unsupported number.
- Private collection is local first. Outward transfer requires the applicable current grant;
  a retained grant alone does not connect a service at startup. Deny and revoke override it.
- Public search uses the admitted published projection. Private search is separate, opt-in and
  scoped to the owner's record. Remote private-agent service acceptance remains open.
- A portfolio is a projection of the record, not a competing store. Preserve owner edits,
  history and undo when automation suggests changes.
- Source collection, first-run setup, hosted account isolation, publication withdrawal,
  native packaging and outside-user acceptance have different completion gates in the
  [release path](.loop/MILESTONES.md).

## Brand Commitments

Use the owner's language: “Your work deserves a record,” “private truth, public view,”
“provenance over presentation,” and “grounded pride.” The experience should show the work and
retained artifacts before its tools. The bipinnate compound leaf is the established mark;
the design system and its implementation values live in `@foliage/tokens` and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
These commitments do not license invented biography, collaborations or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Keep the private record useful before asking the builder to share it.
2. Show what mattered with the work and evidence that support it.
3. Preserve the builder's words, corrections, failed assumptions and unfinished work.
4. Let the owner choose each public view and withdraw it; never infer consent from activity.
5. Make automation legible and reversible without making the user operate its machinery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every work account and cited proof must remain readable without motion, WebGL, hover or a
specific input device. Controls need named actions and visible state. A missing source,
unavailable service or excluded item must have a truthful recovery or explanation, without
reducing the person's work to a score.
