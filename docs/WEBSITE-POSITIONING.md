# Foliage website positioning

Owner direction, 2026-09-10: Foliage builds and maintains the developer or software
engineer’s portfolio. Photosynthesis and Ladybug should make that portfolio useful:
insights connected to the person’s why, evidence of impact, and help promoting the work
that serves their goals. The portfolio’s effectiveness matters as much as its existence.

The public story starts with **You build software. Foliage builds your portfolio.**
A manual portfolio editor does not describe the product. The browser demo isolates one
Leaflet review flow and must explain why the visitor supplies text there.

The longer-term ambition is a foundation for personal AGI, grounded in real work,
personal privacy, control, and alignment. Describe this as a direction, not a claim that
AGI or autonomous career promotion has shipped. Impact claims need evidence; do not
invent customer outcomes, performance multipliers, scores, or rankings.

Employer partnerships should make otherwise invisible contributions visible through
an agreed private-to-public release process. The individual controls their representation;
the employer’s confidentiality and disclosure rules govern company material. The world
Canopy is the public destination for deliberately released Leaves and company Trees.
Do not imply that permission to read private work is permission to publish it.

## The clear signal of emergence

Owner wording, 2026-09-10: **“We are curating the clear signal of emergence.”**
This is the organizing idea beneath the concrete promise that Foliage builds the
portfolio. Real work reveals developing capabilities, impact, and personal direction
over time. Photosynthesis gathers evidence; Ladybug helps the person explore its
meaning and connect it to their why.

The public expression is **See what you’re becoming. Choose what the world sees.**
The private Leaf supports the person’s own understanding first. Deliberate release
makes selected work legible to employers and the world Canopy. Curation must preserve
uncertainty and context; it is not permission to infer a fixed identity, fabricate impact,
or assign a score. Describe capabilities and changes through inspectable work.

## Current claims boundary

- Photosynthesis drafts candidates from authorized evidence. Review governs entry into
  the private Leaf; collection and approval do not publish it.
- Ladybug is the individual agent. The current implementation answers from the supplied
  Leaf and proposes; it cannot approve, promote, publish, share, or change policy.
- Ongoing portfolio maintenance, promotion around personal goals, employer partnership
  onboarding, and the connected world Canopy are the broader direction. Do not sell the
  website demo as these connected services.
- The browser demo holds visitor text in tab memory and shows source review and a reader
  preview. It does not call Photosynthesis or Ladybug, verify authorship, or publish.
